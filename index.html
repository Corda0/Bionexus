About BioNexus

BioNexus is the University of Aveiro's annual conference dedicated to bioinformatics and computational biology. Over two days, students and professionals come together to explore the latest advances in genomic data analysis, machine learning for clinical research, and database design for life sciences.

Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic research and real-world application, creating a space where undergraduate and graduate students can learn directly from experienced researchers and industry professionals.
